## What is STAIRS?

STAIRS is a scoring and planning system designed for smart telescope users. It helps you determine:

- **Target Suitability:** Which astronomical objects are best suited for your specific telescope and sensor.
- **Exposure Optimization:** The ideal individual and total exposure times for a target based on your local light pollution (Bortle scale) and hardware.
- **Timing:** When to image a target for the best Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR).
