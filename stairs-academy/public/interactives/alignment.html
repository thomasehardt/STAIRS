Why EQ Mounts Prevent Field Rotation

The sky rotates around the Celestial Pole as the Earth turns. An Alt-Az mount swings in both altitude and azimuth to follow a target — its axes never align with the sky's rotation, causing field rotation in the image. An Equatorial mount tilts its RA axis to point at the Celestial Pole, so it only needs one axis of motion to track perfectly.
