Narrowband & the Hubble Palette

Narrowband filters isolate specific emission lines from ionized gases in nebulae. H-alpha (656 nm) traces hydrogen, OIII (500 nm) traces doubly ionized oxygen, and SII (672 nm) traces sulfur. The Hubble Palette maps SII → Red, Hα → Green, OIII → Blue for the iconic golden-blue false-color image.
