## The Big Idea

STAIRS is a planning tool that answers one question: **"What should I image tonight, and when?"**

A lot of apps tell you what's in the sky. STAIRS tells you what you should actually **point your telescope at** — ranked by how well the target fits your specific equipment, your sky conditions, and your schedule.

---

## The Problem

Smart telescopes like the Seestar S50, Dwarf II, and Vespera make it easy to _capture_ images. But they leave the _planning_ to you:

- Which targets are visible from my backyard tonight?
- Will this galaxy fit on my sensor, or is it too big/small?
- How long do I need to expose to get a decent image?
- Is the moon going to wash out my target?
- Should I image M42 for an hour, then switch to M31?

Most people end up scrolling through sky charts, guessing, and hoping for the best. STAIRS turns this into a data-driven process.

---

## How STAIRS Works

STAIRS combines three layers of information:

### 1. Target Catalog

A database of thousands of deep-sky objects (NGC, IC, Messier, Barnard, etc.) with:

- Coordinates (RA/Dec)
- Magnitude and surface brightness
- Angular size
- Target type (galaxy, nebula, cluster, etc.)
- Constellation and seasonal visibility

### 2. Your Equipment Profile

A telescope profile includes:

- Aperture and focal length
- Sensor dimensions and pixel pitch
- Mount type (Alt-Az vs equatorial)

STAIRS uses this to compute field of view, pixel scale, and light collection rate.

### 3. Current Conditions

Fetched in real time:

- **Weather**: cloud cover, humidity, temperature, wind, seeing (from Open-Meteo)
- **Light pollution**: your configured Bortle scale
- **Moon**: phase, altitude, position relative to each target
- **Sun**: twilight times (astronomical, nautical, civil)

### The Output

A ranked, scheduled plan:

```
Target              Score  Window          Block
M42 (Orion Nebula)   85    8:00 PM - 10:30 PM  60 min
M31 (Andromeda)     72    8:00 PM - 12:30 AM  60 min
M45 (Pleiades)      68    9:30 PM - 1:30 AM   60 min
```

## Killer Features

### Multi-Night Planning

Most planning apps only tell you what's visible _tonight_. STAIRS scans **up to 14 nights ahead** and scores each one. This means you can plan your entire week:

- See which nights are cloud-free _before_ you get your hopes up
- Know that Wednesday will be your best chance for that dim galaxy (new moon, clear skies)
- Get an alert when a rare target reaches peak altitude later this week

The multi-night forecast shows you, for each night: astronomical twilight start/end, effective dark hours (after moon and clouds), and a quality score color-coded so you can spot the good nights instantly.

### Multi-Site Comparison

If you have multiple observing locations — a light-polluted backyard and a dark mountain site — STAIRS can **compare conditions side by side** and tell you which site will be better on any given night. This is invaluable for deciding whether to make the drive to your dark site.

See the [Multi-Night & Multi-Site Planning](/stairs-docs/06-using-stairs/multi-night/) page for details.

## What STAIRS Is Not

- **Not a telescope controller** — it doesn't slew your mount or capture images
- **Not a planetarium** — it doesn't render the night sky visually (though it uses astroplan under the hood)
- **Not a stacking engine** — it doesn't process your images

STAIRS is a **planning layer** that sits _between_ the catalog and your telescope's app. It tells you _what_ to image and _when_ — your telescope's app handles the actual capture.

## Who Is STAIRS For?

| User Type                     | Value                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New smart telescope owner** | Don't know what to image? STAIRS picks targets that fit your scope.                   |
| **Experienced imager**        | Want to optimize your night? STAIRS schedules targets to maximize usable hours.       |
| **Multi-location observer**   | Have a dark site and a city location? Compare scores and decide where to go.          |
| **Session logger**            | Want to track what you've captured? STAIRS logs your sessions with notes and ratings. |
