## How This Guide Works

Every lesson follows the same structure:

- **The Big Idea** — plain English summary
- **See It** — animated video showing the concept
- **Play With It** — interactive simulation to experiment with
- **The Details** — diagrams, callouts, key terms
- **In STAIRS** — how this concept feeds into the app

Want the equations? Click the **Deep Dive** toggle on any page.

## Who This Is For

- You own (or are considering) a smart telescope like the **Seestar S50**, **Dwarf II**, or **Vespera**
- You want to understand _why_ certain settings work better than others
- You're curious about the science but don't have a physics background
- You want to get the most out of STAIRS for planning your sessions
